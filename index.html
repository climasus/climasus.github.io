About the project

What is ClimaSUS?

ClimaSUS is a scientific software ecosystem for obtaining, integrating, standardizing and analyzing health, climate and environmental data in Brazil.

🗄️
DATASUS
SIM-DO, SIH, SIA, SINAN, SI-PNI, SINASC
🌡️
Dados climáticos
INMET, FIORES, INCT-CONEXÃO, satélite
🗺️
Análise espacial
Malhas IBGE, modelos bayesianos, LISA
📈
Modelos preditivos
DLNM, fração atribuível, bioprognose
